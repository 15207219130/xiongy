I am a PhD student in **Computational Science and Engineering at McMaster University**, supervised by Dr. Elkafi Hassini. My research focuses on optimization under uncertainty and machine learning, with applications in sustainable logistics, renewable energy, and telecommunication networks.

I received my Master of Science in Management Science and Engineering (2025) and Bachelor of Engineering in Safety Engineering (2022) from Zhongnan University of Economics and Law in Wuhan, China.

## Research interests

- **Methodology:** Optimization under uncertainty and machine learning.
- **Applications:** Sustainable logistics, renewable energy, and telecommunication networks.

## Publications and current research

My published work addresses reliable shelter location, emergency response network design, and risks of vehicle electrification in emergency medical transport. My current projects include food-delivery demand forecasting and rider prepositioning, last-mile delivery preferences, reliable optical networks, wind farm design, and solver-guided generation of valid inequalities with large language models.
